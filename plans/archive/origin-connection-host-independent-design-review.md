## Acceptance boundary and alternatives

The selected design normalizes origin through document commands and derives physical views from one graph. A host-only origin config store would make geometry disagree with snapshot/restore and controlled ownership; reject it. Split binding with a hidden global field would have no acknowledgement owner; reject it. One client with protocol-tagged correlation preserves existing literal-v1 callers and additive Runtime reads; a separate client/factory would duplicate owner/disposal state and is unnecessary.

081 acceptance requires the exact amended allowlist, accepted 070 composition, public producer paths in all three hosts, measured readiness, actual DOM/SVG/custom renderer assertions, controlled acknowledgement and versioned restored geometry, installed type/runtime consumers, focused saved scope and combined full/release gates.
