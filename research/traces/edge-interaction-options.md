## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming behavior:** Flow gates selection, focus tab stops, and endpoint reconnection per edge, and sets a custom hit width. Flow's browser tests establish its own output; direct reference parity remains unverified.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow accepts positive finite `interactionWidth`. The reference also permits zero to omit its hit path. Flow uses a button role when selectable and an image role otherwise; the pinned React wrapper chooses group or image by focusability.
- **Unverified:** Reference browser behavior for focusability and reconnection overrides, zero-width hit paths, and a cross-run output comparison remain outside this test boundary.
