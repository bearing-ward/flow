## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming behavior:** Flow emits start/end marker URLs and styled strokes, with its own schema and browser tests. Reference geometry and pixel parity are unverified.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow uses validated marker and stroke fields instead of arbitrary style objects, style strings, custom marker IDs, and all upstream marker parameters.
- **Unverified:** Marker pixels, scaling under zoom or stroke width, dynamic defaults, custom definitions, and animation need separate checks.
