# Plan 180 ownership suite reduction, round 2

## Scope and test boundaries

Round 1 at `7b5ba09` relocated 268 synthetic cases into Vitest Browser Mode and grew the combined test directories to 104,433 lines. Round 2 deletes redundant axis combinations, their oracle tables and their fixture functions. It adds no Node unit tests and changes no production code, public API, telemetry, saved graph data or imported research artifact.

The 70 retained adapter cases still execute real Vanilla and Vue adapters in Chromium through `@vitest/browser-playwright`. Synthetic capture receipts establish callback/resource ownership only. Trusted native input remains in the per-host Playwright contracts. The 15 Vanilla renderer ownership e2e cases and two Foldkit native-control ownership cases remain, for 87 ownership cases total. This count includes every ownership browser file, rather than treating Vitest as outside the browser inventory.
