## React production path

The public `ReactFlow` prop `autoPanOnSelection` defaults to `true` at `packages/react/src/container/ReactFlow/index.tsx:135`. Its type describes panning when the cursor reaches the viewport edge during a selection drag at `packages/react/src/types/component-props.ts:639`. The store supplies the shared `autoPanSpeed` default of 15 at `packages/react/src/store/initialState.ts:140`. `ReactFlow` passes `autoPanOnSelection` through `GraphView` and `FlowRenderer` to `Pane` at `packages/react/src/container/ReactFlow/index.tsx:289`, `packages/react/src/container/GraphView/index.tsx:143`, and `packages/react/src/container/FlowRenderer/index.tsx:123`.

`Pane` starts a rectangle only for a primary left pointer under its selection guards. It captures the pointer, stores the starting point in flow coordinates, and places a zero-size rectangle in the store at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:143`. Once pointer movement exceeds the pane click distance, it clears the previous selection, calls `onSelectionStart`, starts auto-pan once, and updates the rectangle at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:286`.

The auto-pan loop reads the last canvas-relative pointer position and cached container bounds. `calcAutoPan` returns a signed displacement on each axis; the default active edge band is 40 pixels, and the velocity rises toward the edge up to the configured speed at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:259` and `packages/system/src/utils/general.ts:57`. `panBy` adds that screen-space displacement to the viewport transform, subject to the translation extent, and reports whether the transform changed at `packages/react/src/store/index.ts:417` and `packages/system/src/utils/store.ts:510`. A changed transform causes `Pane` to recalculate the rectangle at the same pointer position. The loop schedules another animation frame even when panning did not change the transform at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:265`.

The stored start stays in flow coordinates. On each pointer move or successful auto-pan frame, `commitUserSelectionRect` projects that start through the current viewport and builds a screen-space rectangle between the projected start and the pointer at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:187`. It passes the rectangle and current transform to `getNodesInside`, which converts the bounds back to flow coordinates and checks full or partial node overlap at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:221` and `packages/system/src/utils/graph.ts:264`. `Pane` adds selectable edges connected to selected nodes, emits node and edge selection changes when the ID sets change, and stores the current rectangle at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:227`. `UserSelection` renders that rectangle as an absolute-positioned div at `packages/react/src/components/UserSelection/index.tsx:11`.

Pointer up clears the temporary rectangle, calls `onSelectionEnd` after an actual drag, and cancels the animation frame at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:318`. Pointer cancel and component cleanup also cancel the frame at `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:276` and `packages/react/src/container/Pane/index.tsx:356`.

## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming:** Flow keeps the rectangle origin in flow coordinates and reprojects it after viewport movement. Both Flow and the pinned adapters pan while the pointer remains in a 40-pixel edge band. Flow's browser test verifies viewport movement with a stationary pointer near the edge and publication on release.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow uses a constant 15-pixel step in the edge band and commits selection on pointer release. The pinned adapters ramp velocity within the band and update selection during pointer movement and auto-pan frames. Flow exposes no `autoPanOnSelection` or `autoPanSpeed` option.
- **Unverified:** Flow's browser test does not assert that animation frames stop after release or that selection extends to nodes revealed by panning. The pinned shared Playwright suite lacks a direct selection auto-pan assertion. Direct visual parity remains unverified.
