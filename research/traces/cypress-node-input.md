## Conformity and deviations

Conforming behavior is bounded by the unchanged local Chromium ports and the acceptance results in Plan 135. Node click selection with the false option, independent marquee admission, pane dismissal and conditional selection DOM follow the pinned authority above.

Intentionally different: Vue and Foldkit retain their false select-on-drag defaults; Vanilla retains its enabled default. Fixtures explicitly supply the upstream configuration. Framework lifecycle ownership, controlled acceptance and Effect telemetry remain in their established owners. Non-interactive edge labels, Vanilla shared-SVG edge stacking, Foldkit mounted-hidden toolbars and instance-scoped marker IDs are unchanged.

Unverified: live React execution, other engines, physical devices, Cypress component phase 128, full-suite acceptance and collector-side OTLP delivery. Source similarity alone is not parity evidence.
