# Foldkit selection rectangle readiness race

## Browser entry and observed order

UP-126-022 runs the unchanged shared contract at `apps/shared-e2e/upstream/nodes.ts:23`, registered by `apps/foldkit/e2e/upstream-playwright.e2e.ts:21`. The adapter opens `/upstream/nodes/general` and waits for the Foldkit fixture's ready marker at `apps/shared-e2e/upstream/adapter.ts:187`. The test measures Node-1, presses Shift, and starts its drag 150 pixels left and 25 pixels above that measurement.

The untouched `66a94e2` baseline passes 19/20 repetitions. Two temporary diagnostic runs each pass 48/50. The second run records both failing presses at client `(-150, -25)` targeting `<html>`. Thus the previously measured Node-1 origin was `(0, 0)`. At native Shift keydown, its rendered bounds are already `(550.5590209960938, 72)`. Native Shift, queued `SetCanvasKey`, and the accepted `selectionKeyPressed: true` transition precede the off-canvas press. No `GestureDown` enters the runtime, and no controlled `ReplaceDocument` cancels selection. This refutes the planner's key/acknowledgement hypothesis for the captured failures. Temporary probes are removed; local logs are `/tmp/flow-130-baseline.log`, `/tmp/flow-130-instrumented.log`, and `/tmp/flow-130-instrumented2.log`, with reduced failing traces in `/tmp/flow-130-failure-evidence.json`.

## Production path and fixture error

The product initially fits after measurements and pane dimensions become available at `packages/foldkit/src/application.ts:2165`. Public `createViewportClient.fitView` sends a typed viewport request at `packages/foldkit/src/application.ts:638`. `ViewportRequest` derives a terminal response from accepted model state at `packages/foldkit/src/application.ts:3316`; `RespondViewport` emits it without a DOM-commit barrier at `packages/foldkit/src/application.ts:1111`. This is the documented terminal-status/target-viewport contract, not a rendered-geometry acknowledgement.

At baseline, `apps/foldkit/src/upstream-fixture.ts:145` set readiness after one animation frame. Foldkit 0.169.0 batches rendering and defers a frame while its message queue drains at `node_modules/foldkit/dist/runtime/renderer.js:393`. The marker could therefore expose the initial DOM before the fitted transform committed. This is fixture misuse of the public viewport result, not a defect in modifier ordering. The repair observes the DOM transform returned by the fit operation at `apps/foldkit/src/upstream-fixture.ts:143`, checks its browser-normalized CSS at `apps/foldkit/src/upstream-fixture.ts:152`, and disconnects the observer on completion or pagehide at `apps/foldkit/src/upstream-fixture.ts:260`. It observes the root because Runtime.embed replaces its original container.

Once the press hits the canvas, the existing product path remains unchanged. Native keyboard subscriptions enqueue `SetCanvasKey` at `packages/foldkit/src/canvas-input.ts:47`. Canvas pointerdown enters the native resource at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:7857`. The same FIFO delivers modifier updates and presses, and `GestureDown` chooses selection versus pan at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:4194`. `GestureMoved` updates flow coordinates; the view projects those through the viewport and renders `.flow-foldkit-selection-rect` at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:7914`. Release derives membership with core `selectionInRect` at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:4385`, publishes its controlled proposal, and retains bounds at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:4410`. The fixture accepts through its public action port at `apps/foldkit/src/upstream-fixture.ts:215`; core's measured `graphBounds` feeds the persistent box at `packages/foldkit/src/graph.ts:7820`.

## Other hosts and test boundary

Vanilla applies viewport CSS synchronously at `apps/vanilla/src/mount-flow.ts:1716` and creates its selection rectangle on pointerdown at `apps/vanilla/src/mount-flow.ts:10052`. Vue fits from a post-flush measurement watcher at `packages/vue/src/flow-canvas.ts:5571` and reads Shift from the native pointer event at `packages/vue/src/flow-canvas.ts:2688`. Neither fixture uses Foldkit's one-frame ready marker. Their repeated upstream results are recorded in Plan 130; this source trace alone is not proof against every initial-fit race.

The additional regression at `apps/foldkit/e2e/selection-rect-race.e2e.ts:5` deliberately holds a viewport CSS update while allowing animation frames to run. The baseline incorrectly announces readiness. The repair keeps readiness absent, then announces it after the test releases the real CSS update. Native Shift immediately before pointerdown then produces active and persistent rectangles and selects the first three nodes. This controlled DOM-delay proof is separate from the unchanged upstream repetitions, which use unmodified browser rendering and input. No waits, retries, ledger outcomes, or assertions change in the upstream contract.

## Conformity and deviations

Conforming at the unchanged upstream and added browser regression boundaries: Shift-drag renders the active rectangle, selects the expected nodes, and leaves persistent bounds. Intentionally different internally: Foldkit uses queued typed model updates and batched DOM rendering. The fixture now acknowledges rendered geometry explicitly instead of assuming a terminal model result has painted. Product selection, controlled document ownership, and telemetry remain unchanged. Full-profile acceptance, other engines, live React comparison, and collector-side OTLP delivery remain unverified.
