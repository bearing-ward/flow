## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming behavior:** Flow has a wide transparent hit stroke, focusable group, pointer and keyboard selection, and visible selected/focus state. Flow browser tests establish its own output; direct reference parity remains unverified.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow selects one edge at a time. Cross-node multi-selection, per-edge selectable/focusable overrides, deletion, and configurable interaction width remain separate work.
- **Unverified:** Pinned reference keyboard behavior lacks a direct browser assertion; accessibility testing beyond the focused interaction cases has not run.
