## Conformity and deviations

- Conforming to adopted D1-D8 functional requirements: original shared contracts and production routes support the listed bounded offline outcomes, with dedicated three-host tests and package interoperability checks. C is availability evidence under the catalog definition, not a runtime reference comparison.
- Intentionally different: deterministic local text/image simulation, typed Flow panels, finite DAG branch/join policy, and versioned local saves replace unobserved or service-dependent upstream behavior. These follow adopted decisions. The test boundary is local package, production-route browser, packed-consumer and local room transport acceptance. Presentation groups do not execute, and rooms do not synchronize runs.
- Unverified: licensed subscription source and runtime subscriptions, exact template visuals and coordinates, upstream lock/validation/streaming/persistence/cancellation semantics, commercial models, external service delivery, credentials storage, durable or distributed execution, React/Svelte workflow hosts, and collector-side OTLP delivery. No source-similarity or generic building-block test is treated as proof of these behaviors.
