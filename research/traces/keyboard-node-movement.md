## Flow production and test paths

Core `moveSelectedNodes` creates a grouped flow-space change from visible selected top-level nodes, omitting every node with `draggable: false`. The single and group apply functions reject locked IDs, duplicate IDs, and missing IDs (`packages/core/src/domain/flow-runtime.schema.ts:767-863`).

The vanilla key handler accepts arrows on a focused selected node, computes the configured 5-unit or 20-unit step, applies node extents, and publishes the grouped change. It skips an empty result when all selected nodes are locked, retains focus through `FlowBrowserService.focus`, and pans once when needed to reveal the moved focused node. Keyboard movement does not schedule a frame loop (`apps/vanilla/src/mount-flow.ts:3606-3681`, `apps/vanilla/src/flow-browser.ts:19-49`).

Flow browser tests cover selected group movement, controlled acknowledgement, a locked mixed group, focus retention, one-shot reveal, and that the viewport remains stable after movement (`apps/vanilla/e2e/movement-slices.e2e.ts:77-127`, `apps/vanilla/e2e/movement-slices.e2e.ts:267-325`). These are Flow tests, not direct comparisons against the pinned reference browser suite.

## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforms:** Flow moves movable visible selected nodes by five flow units per arrow keypress, or 20 with Shift, publishes one semantic change, preserves controlled state, and reveals the focused moved node.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow has no global `nodesDraggable` setting or selection wrapper contract. A per-node lock leaves selection and focus enabled and excludes that node from group movement. Modifier combinations with Meta, Control, or Alt are reserved.
- **Unverified:** The pinned reference browser suite lacks a direct keyboard-movement assertion. Screen-reader announcement behavior and a broader accessibility audit remain unverified.
