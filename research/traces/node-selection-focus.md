## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming:** Single and additive selection, selectable and focusable flags, selected classes, keyboard activation, and pane clearing follow the traced reference behavior. Flow browser tests prove the stated vanilla output and controlled acknowledgement.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow publishes one schema-derived selection-set change for additive and rectangle operations. Its plain DOM host owns focus and pointer capture.
- **Unverified:** Auto-pan on focus, arrow-key node movement, and direct visual parity with React or Svelte remain open. The pinned browser suite does not assert those keyboard and focus details.
