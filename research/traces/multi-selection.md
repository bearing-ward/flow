## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming:** Modifier toggles preserve other selection. Shift-drag shows a rectangle and selects fully enclosed, visible, selectable nodes and their selectable connected edges. Controlled callers acknowledge the selection-set change before selected classes appear.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow commits rectangle selection once on pointer release. The pinned adapters update selection while the rectangle moves and can render a persistent selection overlay. Flow currently supports only Full containment and no selection auto-pan.
- **Unverified:** Partial-overlap mode, auto-pan, touch interaction, and direct upstream visual parity lack Flow tests. The pinned Playwright suite also leaves some of those branches unasserted.
