## Conformity and deviations

The unchanged ported contracts observe click selection, persistent measured selection bounds, middle-button pan, and deletion in each host. Round-2 execution results are recorded in Plan 127. Vanilla's threshold-zero selection publication now precedes the drag-start callback, matching `packages/system/src/xydrag/XYDrag.ts:282` and `packages/system/src/xydrag/XYDrag.ts:298`. The native receipt tests that order with pointer capture held. The eight round-1 conflicts use `selectNodesOnDrag: false`; their original assertions remain unchanged and selection is not published at activation or activated release.

Intentionally different architecture: Flow reuses core measured graph bounds and host viewport panning with a non-intercepting bounds overlay. React owns a separate rectangle drag handler. The observed middle-button pan contract is the test boundary for that implementation choice.

Unverified behavior includes precise selection timing in Vue and Foldkit, positive-threshold select-on-drag-start timing, upstream deselection of other nodes when drag selection is disabled, direct primary-button dragging of the bounds rectangle, live-reference parity, other browser engines, full-profile integration acceptance, and the unported Cypress phases 127 and 128. Existing disabled-option reentry tests preserve Flow's current deselection behavior; this bounded repair does not establish parity for it. No OpenTelemetry collector delivery is claimed; existing selection, edit, and lifecycle spans remain the observability boundary.
