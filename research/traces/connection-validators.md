## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforms:** A caller-defined boolean rule can reject a candidate before an edge is added or reconnected. Flow's browser tests prove both published-change and rendered-edge behavior for rejection; the pinned source establishes xyflow's corresponding API and runtime path. The pinned automated suites inspected here do not assert a custom rule, so direct parity is unverified.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow keeps document integrity in the Effect core and invokes one host-level callback at commit time for pointer, click, and reconnection. It catches thrown callbacks and treats them as rejection. The pinned drag path evaluates validity during movement, renders a `valid` handle class, and permits a local handle rule to replace the flow-level rule. Flow chooses one host rule to keep framework lifecycle and callback ownership in `apps/`; its test boundary is the published semantic change and rendered edge.
- **Unverified:** Live callback replacement, local handle precedence, rejection styling during hover, and programmatic edge insertion remain outside the test boundary here. Flow's exception tests prove rejection and later recovery in the browser; they do not prove telemetry export or behavior for every exception source. The pinned automated suites inspected here do not establish a custom-validator result either.
