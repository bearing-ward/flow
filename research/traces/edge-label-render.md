## Conformity and deviations

- **Conforming behavior:** Flow anchors labels to the straight midpoint, Bezier half-parameter point, and routed segment midpoint, with core and browser tests for its output. Reference output parity remains unverified.
- **Intentionally different:** Flow renders noninteractive HTML DIV labels in `.flow-edge-labels`; React measures SVG text inside the selectable edge group, with `pointer-events: all` on its text wrapper at `packages/system/src/styles/init.css:160`. Svelte portals HTML. Flow keeps built-in labels from intercepting canvas input. The test boundary is `apps/vanilla/e2e/renderer-contract.e2e.ts:164`; UP-126-014 therefore cannot establish stroke hit-width parity when its fixed screen offset lands on the upstream label outside the scaled interaction stroke.
- **Unverified:** Pixel placement against the pinned adapters, rich content, background padding, collision handling, and general label-interaction parity beyond UP-126-014 remain outside this slice.
